Jointl offers several supported ways to connect. Use the narrowest access method that
fits the job, request only the required scope, and keep every authorization within the
member's current role, company access, and record visibility.

Continue with [choosing access](/getting-started/choose-access), then follow the relevant REST,
MCP, Zapier, or Greenhouse guide.
